# Decision Wheel

Spin for lunch, a focus task or who pays. Ten decks cover everyday choices and games with friends, including yes or no, coin, dice, coffee and lunch.

Turn the wheel to choose a deck. Press OK or Start to spin. The result is drawn once at the start, then the reel slows and settles on it. Press Back during a spin to cancel without recording the result.

The back shows the options, the odds on weighted decks and recent results. The app remembers the last result for each deck. Sound and light cues are optional.
