# Pour Over

A pour-over timer that shows the path and target weight for your next pour while you hold the kettle. Choose from 11 V60 and Kalita Wave recipes, including Copenhagen, several 4:6 variations and an iced flash brew.

The wheel selects the recipe and adjusts the dose from 10 to 40 g. The front shows the pour path, time left in the step and cumulative weight for your scale. The back shows grind, temperature and the next instruction.

Press Start to pause or resume a brew, or OK to advance a step. The pour and stop sounds make it easy to follow the timer by ear. Use any kitchen scale to check the targets.
