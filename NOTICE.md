## Artwork and content

- **Oracle** includes traditional sayings, attributed adaptations and three brief excerpts from Richard M. Gummere’s translation of Seneca’s *Moral Letters to Lucilius*. Card credits appear in the app. [Gummere translation](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Moral_letters_to_Lucilius)
- **Pour Over** credits its recipe sources, including Coffee Collective, James Hoffmann, Tetsu Kasuya, Scott Rao and Matt Winton. The presets adapt their methods into timer steps. [Coffee Collective’s Kalita Wave guide](https://coffeecollective.dk/pages/brew-guide/kalita-wave)

The apps also use the BUSY Bar’s firmware-provided assets by name. Build dependencies retain the licenses provided by their packages.
