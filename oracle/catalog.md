# Oracle

One card a day for a change of perspective. Seven decks hold 217 cards, from Latin sayings and Stoic ideas to engineering laws, reflective questions and notes on applied AI.

Turn the wheel to choose a deck, or press OK or Start to draw another card. The front reveals each card in short pages. The back shows the full text and source.

Each deck has its own note as you turn the wheel. The reading pace and sounds are adjustable, and the app remembers your last deck.
