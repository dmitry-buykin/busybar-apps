# Rubber Duck

A rubber duck that asks you a question when you and your coding agents get stuck. Use it to clarify a brief, review a change or decide what to check next.

Turn the wheel to choose from 14 decks with 123 prompts, including debugging, verification and applied AI. Press Start for a question. Press OK and the duck raises a wing to explain why it matters. The back shows the full prompt and explanation.

Each deck remembers your place. Set the reading pace and choose how often the duck quacks. The questions are built in and available offline.
