# Ship It!

Celebrate finishing a piece of work with a miniature rocket launch. Pick a simulated deployment target, press Start and watch the countdown zoom in for ignition before the camera pulls back for liftoff.

There are seven targets, from Dev to Friday. Canary rolls out in stages, and Rollback lands the last release on the pad. You can abort a countdown or rollout, or turn launch risks off in Settings.

The back shows a fictional deploy log, recent launches and a streak of shipping days. The whole simulation runs on the bar.
